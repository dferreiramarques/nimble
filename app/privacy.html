Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

nimble (nimble.monco.io) is a tool to manage a product in one file: notes, a roadmap, diagrams and task boards. This page explains what happens to your data.

What we do not do

nimble has no database and does not store your product. Your data goes from your browser to your own Google Drive and nowhere else. We do not sell, share or analyse your data, and we use no advertising or tracking cookies.

What nimble accesses in your Google account

  • Your name, email and profile picture, shown in the app so you can see which account is signed in. They are read in your browser and are not stored by us.
  • Files in your Google Drive that nimble creates, or that you open with nimble (scope drive.file). This is where your product file is saved. nimble cannot see any other file in your Drive.

nimble uses this access only to load and save your product file when you ask or when you edit. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What our server handles

  • Sign-in: to keep you signed in, the server keeps a Google refresh token in an encrypted cookie in your browser. The server itself does not store it.
  • Logs: our hosting provider (Railway) may keep standard request logs, such as IP addresses, for security and operation.

In your browser

nimble keeps a local working copy of your product and your preferences in your browser storage so it works offline. Signing out or clearing site data removes it.

Your control

  • Delete your product by deleting its file in Google Drive.
  • Revoke nimble at any time in your Google Account permissions.
  • Export your product as a file from the app whenever you want.
